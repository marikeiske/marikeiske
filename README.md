# Hi, I'm Mariana Keiske 👋

M.Sc. student in Computer Engineering at the **Aeronautics Institute of Technology (ITA)**, Brazil, and FAPESP research fellow at **FlyMov**, the Engineering Research Center for Future Air Mobility.

I study how to run AI and safety-critical workloads on **heterogeneous embedded platforms** (CPUs, GPUs and FPGAs) using high-level programming models such as **SYCL, OpenCL and HLS**, with a focus on avionics (DO-178C / DO-254).

## Research

- **Evaluation of High-Level Programming Models for Critical Heterogeneous Avionics** (with D. S. Loubach), submitted to the *2027 IEEE Aerospace Conference*. Three flight-autonomy kernels in native C++, OpenCL and SYCL; the SYCL layer adds only 11–13 µs per launch, while runtime choice changes performance by up to 13.5×.
- **Latency analysis in IoT data transmission for advanced manufacturing**, *Int. J. Adv. Manuf. Technol.*, 2026. [doi:10.1007/s00170-026-18369-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00170-026-18369-y)
- **Early detection of leaf diseases in rice plantations with CNNs**, *IEEE INDUSCON*, 2025.
